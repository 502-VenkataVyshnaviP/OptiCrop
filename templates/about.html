Opti Crop Smart Agricultural Optimization Using ML

The project "Opti Crop: Smart Agricultural Production Optimization Engine" aims to develop an advanced software system that utilizes data-driven insights to optimize agricultural production for different crops. By integrating key environmental factors such as Nitrogen (N), Phosphorous (P), Potassium (K) levels, soil temperature, humidity, pH, rainfall, and crop types, Opti Crop seeks to provide intelligent recommendations to farmers for maximizing yields and resource efficiency.
